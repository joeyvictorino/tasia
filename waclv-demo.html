A message from our leadership✦

Citizen diplomacy since 2004

For more than two decades, WACLV has created opportunities for Southern Nevadans and international visitors to meet, exchange ideas and build lasting understanding.

This work is possible because local professionals, educators, members, hosts, volunteers and partners choose to participate.

People to people

Professional exchange becomes durable understanding when visitors and local leaders meet as peers.

Rooted in Southern Nevada

WACLV brings the region's knowledge, institutions and hospitality into the global conversation.
